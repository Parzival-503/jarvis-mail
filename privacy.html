Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Jarvis Mail is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner, on their own computer. This policy explains how it handles data from Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Jarvis Mail reads, labels, archives, deletes and drafts email in the owner's own Gmail accounts, using Google's Gmail API.

Where data goes

Email data is processed on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer. Jarvis Mail does not run a server that collects data, and it does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. To decide what is important or to draft a reply, short excerpts of a message may be sent to an AI model service chosen by the owner, solely to perform that task for the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Jarvis Mail's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and can delete the stored tokens from their computer.
