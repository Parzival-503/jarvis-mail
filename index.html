Jarvis Mail

A private mail helper for one person.

Jarvis Mail is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It connects to its owner's own email accounts so it can sort mail, archive or delete what isn't needed, and prepare reply drafts for the owner to review. It never sends an email without the owner's explicit approval.

Jarvis Mail is not offered to the public and has no other users.
